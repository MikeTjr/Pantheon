Jesus — Center of All Creation

In this concordance Jesus is presented as the central focal point of Christian cosmology and as a primary reference node in cross-traditional correspondences. Entries that map Christic analogues, typological prefigurations, or comparative motifs (dying-and-rising savior, divine Logos, world-soul mediator) are linked to this canonical node so researchers can trace parallels and divergences clearly.

Project Purpose

Pantheon Concordance is a structured research database mapping the intersection of eleven major religious, mythological, and esoteric traditions. It compiles historical syncretism — Roman Interpretatio romana, Patristic reframing of pagan deities, and Islamic-Hermetic correspondences — alongside grimoiric lineages, Kabbalistic systems, and planetary correspondences.

The goal is a unified, searchable network of correspondences: the kind of cross-reference system used by Renaissance occultists like Agrippa and Ficino, and by modern comparative mythology researchers.

Methodology

Three principles govern every entry:

1. Source transparency. Every correspondence is tagged with the tradition and text that makes the claim. The database distinguishes between ancient explicit syncretisms, structural parallels identified by modern scholars, and polemical equivalences made by one tradition about another.

2. Contested claims are preserved, not resolved. Where scholars disagree — on Enochian identification, Goetic derivations, Islamic Isra'iliyyat — both positions are stored and flagged. The database does not adjudicate.

3. This is history of ideas, not theology. Mapping Augustine's claim that Zeus is a demon does not endorse that theology. Every tradition is treated with equal scholarly care and none as uniquely authoritative.
